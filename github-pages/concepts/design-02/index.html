The Evergreen approach

Is electrolysis right for me?

Permanent care,
one follicle at a time.

Electrolysis uses a very fine probe and controlled electrical current to treat the growth source of one hair at a time. Because it does not rely on pigment, it can be used across natural hair colors and skin tones.

Hair grows in cycles, so a complete plan takes consistency rather than promises. Your consultation turns the area, density, prior hair-removal habits, skin response, and schedule into a realistic treatment cadence.

Before & after

Small habits support
a thoughtful treatment.

Before your visit
  • Pause tweezing, waxing, and threading so a visible hair can be treated.
  • Arrive with clean skin and enough visible growth for treatment.
  • Share relevant products, prescriptions, recent procedures, and skin changes.
After your visit
  • Temporary redness or irritation can occur for several hours.
  • Keep the area clean and follow the aftercare discussed at your appointment.
  • Contact a healthcare professional about severe or unexpected symptoms.

Good questions welcome

A few things
worth knowing.

Why does electrolysis require more than one visit?+

Hair grows in cycles, and only visible hairs can be treated at a given appointment. Consistent visits allow different hairs to be treated as they enter a treatable stage.

Does it work on light or gray hair?+

Yes. Electrolysis treats individual follicles rather than targeting pigment, so it can be used on blonde, red, gray, and white hair as well as darker hair.

How are sessions scheduled?+

Appointments are reserved by time. Your recommended session length and current rate are confirmed before booking, based on the area, density, skin response, and your schedule.

Do you offer laser hair removal?+

No. Evergreen Electrolysis is focused on licensed, probe-based electrolysis. Laser treatment is outside a California electrologist’s scope of practice.
